The Key Plans and Tiles assembly taxonomy. A Tile is a composable floor plate segment that combines Private Office, Professional Office, and Corporate Office key plans into standardised building-width increments. Two standard sizes: Small (2,700 SF / 250.84 m²) and Large (4,900 SF / 455.22 m²). Tiles compose to fill the 20,000 SF net leasable floor plate. Design principle: maximise climate zone autonomy — one Tile equals one HVAC zone — while maintaining rolling efficiency as tenant mix changes. See also Floor Plate Standards for the size hierarchy Tiles compose into.
