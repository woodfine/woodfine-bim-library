Climate Zones — where a building sits determines what its envelope must perform to.

Climate zone tokens anchored to the ASHRAE 90.1-2022 climate zone taxonomy (Zones 0–8, subtypes A/B/C). U-values listed are high-performance targets (Passivhaus / NZEB level); ASHRAE 90.1 minimum thresholds are less restrictive than the values encoded here. See ASHRAE 90.1 Tables 5.5-0 through 5.5-8 for code-minimum U-values per zone.
