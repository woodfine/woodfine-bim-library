Furniture — the manufacturer SKUs and regulatory clearances that set a Key Plan's zone depths.

Real manufacturer furniture SKUs and cited regulatory clearances that derive the Habitat, Magazine, and Corridor depths used throughout the Key Plans and Tiles system — the missing foundation that shows where a zone-depth number actually comes from, rather than presenting it as a bare result. Every dimension traces to a real product spec sheet or a cited code clause (ArbStättV, ASR A1.8, ADA 2010, IBC 2021, EN 12464-1). See also Building Width Calculator for the zone-depth tokens these SKUs derive, and Interior for placeable furniture BIM Objects used in Key Plan authoring.
