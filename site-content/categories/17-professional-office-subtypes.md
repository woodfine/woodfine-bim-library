Professional Office Subtypes — the five specialised variants of the Professional Office use-type.

The taxonomy and classification of the five Professional Office sub-types — Medical, Business, Laboratory, Academic, and Civic — and their variants. Sub-types are the leasable-office categories within a Professional Centre or Suburban Office floor plate, each occupying a 1/4 floor tile (5,000 SF / 464.52 m²) or a combination thereof. Area measurements for each sub-type live in the Building Width Calculator category; this category captures taxonomy, codes, and specialisation variants.
