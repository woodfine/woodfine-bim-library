Zone-depth BIM Objects for every leasable use type, anchored to the Key Plans and Tiles spatial taxonomy. Every use type is broken into three zones: Zone 1 Habitat (desks, European Lighting Standard 6m minimum), Zone 2 Magazine (storage beyond Habitat), and Zone 3 Corridor (bilateral access; mirrored floor plate). Metre values are authoritative; feet equivalents travel alongside them.
