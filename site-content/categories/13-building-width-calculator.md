Building Width Calculator — the engine that derives a building's width from its zone depths, not the other way around.

Zone-depth records for every leasable use type, anchored to the Key Plans and Tiles spatial taxonomy — a space-side record, not a product; see "The BIM Object/Space Hierarchy." Every use type is broken into three zones: Zone 1 Habitat (desks, European Lighting Standard 6m minimum), Zone 2 Magazine (storage beyond Habitat), and Zone 3 Corridor (bilateral access; mirrored floor plate). The underlying perimeter/core/circulation logic is well-established in general space-planning practice (ASHRAE, BCO Guide to Specification, LEED/WELL daylight-zone provisions); the three-zone system and names here are Woodfine's own operationalisation of it. Metre values are authoritative; feet equivalents travel alongside them.
